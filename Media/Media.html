Journey

  • Project Vision

    This was my starting point into applied artificial intelligence. Project Vision focused on computer vision–based automation in academic environments, including facial recognition for attendance, attention tracking, and behavioral analysis. Through this project, I gained hands-on experience with real-time vision pipelines, machine learning integration, and data visualization dashboards—laying the technical foundation for my future work.

  • CivicEye

    CivicEye marked a shift from classroom-level solutions to community-oriented intelligence systems. The project explored how AI could assist in public safety and civic monitoring by detecting suspicious activities and presenting meaningful insights through structured dashboards. CivicEye challenged me to think about scalability, system reliability, and ethical responsibility alongside technical accuracy.

  • ParallaxAI

    ParallaxAI is my most ambitious project and represents a conceptual leap in my journey. It is a human-AI co-governance platform designed to ensure that AI decisions remain transparent, auditable, and ethically aligned. Instead of focusing on a single model, ParallaxAI integrates cognitive agents, governance rules, human oversight, and audit chains—reflecting my transition from building AI features to designing responsible AI ecosystems.

Looking Ahead

From intelligent vision systems to ethical governance frameworks, my work continues to move toward building AI that is useful, accountable, and human-centered.
