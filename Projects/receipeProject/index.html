Dosa

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Urad Dal
  • Fenugreek Seeds
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Boiled Potatoes (for masala)

Steps

  1. Soak rice and urad dal for 6 hours.
  2. Grind into smooth batter and ferment overnight.
  3. Heat a pan and spread batter thinly.
  4. Add oil and cook until golden.
  5. Serve hot with chutney and sambar.

Biryani

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Chicken (or veg mix)
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Spices (garam masala, turmeric, chili)
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Coriander & mint
  • Oil / Ghee
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 min.
  2. Fry onions until golden brown.
  3. Add chicken/veg and spices, cook till done.
  4. Layer rice over the cooked mix.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat (dum) 20–25 min.
  6. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.
